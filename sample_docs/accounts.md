# Acme Cloud Accounts

## Resetting your password

To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter
your account email. You will receive a reset link valid for 60 minutes. The
link can be used once; request a new one if it expires.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled from Settings > Security. We
support authenticator apps and SMS codes. Keep your recovery codes somewhere
safe: if you lose access to your second factor, support will ask for a
recovery code before disabling 2FA.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and removes all workspaces you own. To delete
your account, go to Settings > Danger zone and confirm with your password.
Export anything you need first, because deleted data cannot be recovered.
