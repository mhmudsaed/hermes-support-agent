# Acme Cloud Billing

## Refund policy

You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. To request a refund, open
the Billing page, choose the invoice, and click "Request refund". Refunds are
issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

If more than 30 days have passed, contact support — a partial credit may still
be possible for annual plans.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from Settings > Plan. Upgrades apply
immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current
billing cycle, and the new lower price applies from the next invoice.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry the charge automatically after 3 days and again
after 7 days. Update your card on the Billing page to avoid interruption. After
two failed retries the workspace is paused, but no data is deleted for 60 days.
