# Acme Cloud Support Hours

## Contacting support

Live chat support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (UTC+3). Outside
these hours, leave a message and the on-call engineer replies within 24 hours.

For urgent outages on annual plans, call the priority line listed on your
invoice. Free-plan questions are answered in the community forum within
2 business days.

## Response times

Starter plans get a first response within 48 hours. Pro plans get a first
response within 8 business hours. Enterprise plans get a first response within
1 hour, day or night.
